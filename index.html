        Restore demo data

        This replaces products, sales, shipments, and stock adjustments with the original sample. Your display name, business name, and email stay as they are, and you remain signed in.

        What this demo does not do

        Records stay in localStorage on this browser. The password itself is not saved. Only a salted hash is saved, and that check still runs in the browser. A real multi-user version needs a backend API, a database, server-side authorization, password hashing, and separate data for each business. Google sign-in also needs a server to verify Google's token.
